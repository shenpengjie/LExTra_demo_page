Sound Demo of LExTra on WHAMR!

P. Shen, S. He, X. Zhang, Z.-Q. Wang"LExTra: Folded Prompt and Split-Role Attention for Target Speaker Extraction", in ICASSP, 2026.

This demo presents the enhancement results of 20 randomly-selected test signals.
